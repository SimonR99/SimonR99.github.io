Simon Roy

I work on robot learning: world models, language-conditioned planning and vision-language reward models.

I am a PhD student at Polytechnique Montréal and Mila, advised by Giovanni Beltrame. I am also co-founder and CTO of Aquilix, where we put computer vision to work on construction and industrial sites.

Company

Co-founder and CTO, since July 2025

Aquilix builds self-contained AI cameras that detect risks and critical events in real time on construction sites, rail yards and remote sites. A unit installs in a few hours and runs over LTE, with no site internet needed.

  • Lead the technology end to end: camera systems, on-device vision models and the analytics platform.
  • Turn research in computer vision and vision-language models into systems that run unattended in the field.

Projects

  • Goat Brain

    A speech-to-action agent for a mobile robot: a LoRA-tuned LLM with retrieval turns spoken instructions into ROS 2 behavior trees, with a visual interface to follow what the robot is doing.

    CodeVideo

  • Shepherd

    Open-vocabulary semantic mapping for a mobile robot. Segments and captions objects with FastSAM, CLIP and BLIP, then answers natural-language queries over the map.

    Code

  • Search-and-rescue robot autonomy

    Club Capra, ÉTS

    Autonomy stack for a tracked search-and-rescue robot: ROS 2 architecture in Docker, 3D SLAM, exploration, object tracking, arm inverse kinematics and Gazebo simulation. Best in class for search and rescue at EnRicH 2023; competed at ELROB 2024.

    Club Capra

  • Entangled Explorer
